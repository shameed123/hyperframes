# Google I/O 2026 Update Visual Style

## Style Prompt

Build a bright AI keynote motion-graphics short: luminous light canvas, crisp glass UI panels, 3D cards flying through depth, connected agent graphs, and fast editorial transitions. The avatar video alternates between a bottom-right picture-in-picture crop and larger feature windows so the viewer keeps reconnecting with the speaker. The palette should feel like Google AI energy without copying Google branding directly: saturated blue, hot pink, and vivid orange on a warm off-white base.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#fbfaf7`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Ink: `#15161f`
- Muted text: `#5d6270`
- Blue accent: `#2458ff`
- Pink accent: `#ff3aa8`
- Orange accent: `#ff8a21`
- Soft line: `#dfe5f2`

## Typography

- Display: `Archivo Black`, fallback `Arial Black`, for large section titles.
- Technical/UI: `IBM Plex Mono`, fallback `Consolas`, for labels, chips, code, and agent lanes.
- Body/UI: `Helvetica`, fallback `Arial`, for compact explanatory text.

## Motion

- Primary transition: fast diagonal color wipe, 0.42s, blue/pink/orange bands.
- Accent transition: brief overexposure flash at topic changes.
- Elements enter via 3D translate, rotation, scale, and staggered opacity reveals.
- Keep ambient motion finite and subtle: cards float, lanes advance, graph links pulse.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use a dark cyberpunk palette.
- Do not use static slide-like scenes.
- Do not use rounded marketing blobs or decorative bokeh.
- Do not keep the avatar in one position for the whole video.
