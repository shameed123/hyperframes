# Visual Style

## Style Prompt

Light editorial AI-news motion graphics with glossy frosted glass panels, a soft white and ice-blue canvas, subtle circuit-grid detail, and restrained blue-primary energy with pink and orange accents. The presenter remains the human anchor; graphics should support the argument, not overpower the talking head. When website B-roll is featured, the presenter moves to a small bottom-right PiP while a large phone-frame scroll of Anthropic's source page becomes the visual evidence.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#f7fbff`
- Ink: `#101827`
- Muted ink: `#566174`
- Primary blue: `#2468ff`
- Accent pink: `#ed3aa8`
- Accent orange: `#ff8a24`
- Glass: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`

## Typography

- Headlines: `"Archivo Black", "Arial Black", Arial, sans-serif`
- Data and labels: `"IBM Plex Mono", Consolas, monospace`
- Body and captions: `"Aptos", "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif`

## Motion

- Glossy cards enter with short depth, slide, and blur-reveal cues.
- Website scrolls should feel like fast mobile reading: 8-12 seconds across meaningful sections.
- Background motion stays low contrast and slow so it does not compete with the face.
- PiP transitions should feel smooth and editorial, with drop-shadow and rounded corners.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use dark cyberpunk backgrounds.
- Do not cover the presenter's face with captions or large cards.
- Do not rely on remote website loading at render time.
- Do not use graph-paper panels as the primary B-roll when the source website is available.
- Do not make the palette read as one-note blue; keep pink and orange as small active accents.
