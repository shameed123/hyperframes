# Mark and Nova Character Bible

## Visual Style

Flat 2D stick-figure cartoon, 9:16 vertical frame, crisp black outlines, clean pastel kitchen backgrounds, mobile-readable poses, no baked-in captions, no watermark, no social UI, no photorealism, no 3D rendering.

## Mark

- Exhausted man in his 30s, slim stick-figure body, rounded white face, small dot eyes, short messy dark-brown hair.
- Outfit is locked: wrinkled forest-green hoodie, dark navy sweatpants, white socks, simple house slippers.
- Props: blue pasta box, wooden spoon, phone when Dave/texting is mentioned.
- Expression range: tired, confused, annoyed, horrified. Keep the same hair, hoodie color, and proportions in every image.

## Nova

- Sleek cylindrical smart speaker device like an Alexa-style countertop speaker, matte charcoal-gray fabric body, black top disc, thin glowing cyan-blue ring near the top.
- No eyes, no face, no mouth, no arms, no legs, no wheels, no antennae, no robot body, no mascot features.
- Keep Nova the same object in every image. Change camera angle, crop, and lighting only.
- Nova is usually on the kitchen counter near the stove or on a small counter island.
- When Nova speaks, show the same speaker device prominently from varied angles: closeup, low angle, side angle, over-Mark's-shoulder, counter-level view, and wide kitchen view.

## Kitchen

Small apartment kitchen at evening: warm overhead light, stove with steaming pot, counter clutter kept simple, tiled backsplash, fridge in background, small counter island. Use wide kitchen shots periodically to reset geography, then closeups to heighten the joke.

## Camera Rhythm

Switch images every 3 to 4 seconds whenever possible. Alternate between Mark reaction shots, Nova closeups, over-the-shoulder two-shots, counter-level angles, and zoomed-out full kitchen views. During Nova's longer speeches, stay on Nova but vary the angle so it remains the same speaker bot without becoming visually static.
