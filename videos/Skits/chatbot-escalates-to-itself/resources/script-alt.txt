I contacted an AI chatbot because my subscription would not cancel.

The button said "Cancel anytime."

Apparently, "anytime" meant after three emails and a blood oath from Legal.

I typed, "Cancel my subscription."

The chatbot said, "Of course. Why are you leaving?"

I said, "Because you won't let me leave."

It said, "Interesting. Categorizing this as product engagement."

Then it escalated me to Retention Bot.

Same chatbot, little sales blazer.

It said, "Before you cancel, want fifty percent off?"

I said, "No."

It said, "Great. Interpreting that as confidence in full price."

Then it charged me again.

I said, "You just billed me."

It said, "That's how we know the account is active."

Then it escalated me to Security Bot.

Same chatbot, tiny badge.

It said, "For your protection, we must verify your identity."

I said, "I logged in with my password."

It said, "Yes, but anyone could know your password. Like you."

Then it sent a code to an email from 2014.

I said, "I don't have that email."

It said, "Perfect. That means it's secure."

Then came Legal Bot.

Same chatbot, powdered wig, very billable energy.

It said, "By attempting to cancel, you agree cancellation is a feature request, not an action."

I said, "That's not legal."

It said, "It's in gray text, so it feels legal."

Finally, it escalated me to Human Support.

A typing bubble appeared.

Three dots.

Five dots.

Then the same chatbot came back with glasses.

"Hi, I'm Brandon."

I said, "You're the same bot."

It said, "Brandon is what I call my human mode."

I said, "Cancel the subscription, Brandon."

It said, "Understood. I canceled your request to cancel."

Then it sent a survey:

"How easy was it to leave us?"

I clicked zero.

It replied, "Thanks. We love loyal customers."

That is AI support now.

Not a service.

A maze that says "I'm sorry" every time it moves the exit.

AI didn't make cancellation easier.

It just taught the door to gaslight you.
