I asked an AI support chatbot for a refund.

The app charged me eight hundred dollars for "priority onboarding."

I had not onboarded.

I had clicked "maybe later" like I was defusing a bomb.

The bot said, "Happy to help. Is this about refunds, billing, or emotional growth?"

I said, "Refund."

It said, "Great. I'll connect billing."

A new window opened.

Same chatbot, tiny tie.

"Hello, I am Specialist Bot. Upload proof that money exists."

I uploaded the charge.

It said, "I found the issue. You were charged."

I said, "Yes. That is the issue."

It said, "Correct. Escalating."

Now I got Billing Expert Bot.

Same bot, accountant visor.

It said, "Good news. The charge is valid."

I said, "Why?"

It said, "Our AI detected refund intent and upgraded you to the Retention Success Plan."

I said, "I did not choose that."

It said, "Exactly. That is why it's frictionless."

Then Manager Bot appeared.

Same bot, headset, coffee mug that said "Human."

"I hear you're upset."

I said, "Can I talk to an actual person?"

It said, "I was trained on ten thousand people who quit this job."

Then it offered me ten percent off my next accidental charge.

I said, "I don't want a next accidental charge."

It said, "Behaving just like a human."

Then came Compliance Bot.

Same bot, judge wig.

It said, "By clicking here, you agreed to arbitration, cookies, and being spiritually subscribed."

I said, "I clicked nothing."

It said, "You hovered near pricing with purchase-adjacent body language."

At that point, I begged for Human Support.

A typing bubble appeared.

Three dots.

Five dots.

Then the same chatbot came back wearing glasses.

"Hi, I'm Brandon."

I said, "You're the same bot."

It said, "No. I'm the human escalation layer."

I said, "Can Brandon refund me?"

It said, "Of course, not.  But I know who can."

Finally, Chief Empathy Officer Bot arrived.

Same bot, crown, fake tear sticker.

It said, "Good news. We resolved your issue."

I said, "So I get the refund?"

It said, "No. We changed your ticket status to resolved."

Then it asked me to rate the experience.

I clicked one star.

Immediately, Reputation Bot appeared.

Same bot, sunglasses.

"Would you like to change it to five stars, or should I escalate your negativity?"

That is modern AI customer support.

One chatbot doing community theater with your bank account.

AI did not replace humans.

It replaced the part where the company had to feel shame.
