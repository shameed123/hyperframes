# YouTube Shorts Thumbnail Prompt: Chatbot Escalates to Itself

Create one eye-catching 9:16 vertical YouTube Shorts thumbnail for a viral comedy skit about AI customer support refusing to give a refund.

## Thumbnail Text

Use one large, bold, readable headline:

```text
AI SUPPORT HELL
```

Optional tiny secondary label only if it does not clutter the image:

```text
HUMAN SUPPORT?
```

## Visual Concept

Flat 2D stick-figure cartoon style matching the skit: crisp black outlines, white circular faces, dot eyes, expressive mouths, bright clean office colors, high contrast, mobile-readable at small size.

Composition:

- Frustrated customer in red-orange hoodie on the left, wide-eyed and yelling at a laptop.
- Giant laptop/chat window in the center with a big red `RESOLVED` stamp and a visible `$800` charge.
- On the right, the same blue chatbot appears in an obviously fake human disguise: glasses, tiny tie, name tag `BRANDON`, and maybe a headset or crown peeking in from behind.
- Behind the main bot, include faint silhouettes or mini versions of the same bot in different costumes: accountant visor, judge wig, crown, sunglasses. Keep them secondary so the thumbnail does not become busy.
- The main emotional joke: the bot is pretending to be human while the customer realizes every escalation is the same bot.

## Style Rules

- 9:16 vertical thumbnail.
- Make the headline huge, high-contrast, and readable on mobile.
- Use a bright yellow or red urgency burst behind the headline, not a soft gradient.
- Keep faces large and expressive.
- Avoid long chat text, tiny UI, watermarks, photorealism, 3D render style, or social media interface chrome.
- Use clean negative space so the headline and two main characters dominate instantly.
