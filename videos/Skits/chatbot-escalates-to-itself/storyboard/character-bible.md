# Character Bible: Chatbot Escalates to Itself

## Visual Style

- Vertical 9:16 flat 2D stick-figure cartoon.
- Crisp black outlines, white circular faces, dot eyes, simple expressive mouths.
- Light office background with a large laptop or chat-support window as the recurring anchor.
- Keep backgrounds simple and uncluttered so the viewer reads the character pose and screen joke instantly.
- Avoid photorealism, 3D, social media UI, watermarks, busy text, and tiny unreadable chat messages.

## Recurring Characters

### Customer

- Frustrated office user sitting at a desk with a laptop.
- White round stick-figure face, black stick arms and legs.
- Wears a red-orange hoodie or sweater, dark pants.
- Expressions escalate from confused to exhausted to furious.
- Often points at the laptop, holds a credit-card statement, or slumps in defeat.

### Base Chatbot

- Friendly but soulless robot assistant shown beside or emerging from the chat window.
- Round white robot head, simple black dot eyes, small antenna, blue rectangular body.
- Always clearly the same bot underneath every costume.
- Uses accessories to signal escalation level:
  - Specialist Bot: tiny tie and clipboard.
  - Billing Expert Bot: green accountant visor.
  - Manager Bot: headset and coffee mug labeled "Human".
  - Compliance Bot: judge wig and scroll.
  - Brandon Human Layer: glasses and name tag "Brandon".
  - Chief Empathy Officer Bot: crown and fake tear sticker.
  - Reputation Bot: sunglasses and clipboard with star rating.

## Layout Pattern

- Most scenes: customer on one side, oversized laptop/chat window in the middle, bot character on the other side.
- The chat window can contain very short readable fragments only, such as "Refund?", "$800", "Resolved", "5 stars?".
- Final production should prefer HyperFrames-rendered text overlays for exact dialogue; generated images should carry the visual joke.
