# YouTube Shorts Thumbnail Prompt: Vibe Coder Skit

## Concept

Compress the investor-demo punchline into one high-contrast vertical image. The
topic must be obvious before the viewer reads the smaller visual details: vibe
coding failed, the app is broken, the investor has been charged `$4,000`, and
the vibe coder is still proud of it.

## On-Image Hook

`VIBE CODING FAIL`

## Final Image Generation Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: click-focused 9:16 vertical YouTube Shorts thumbnail  
Input images: `../resources/sample-art.png` is the style reference; `../assets/storyboard/full/character-lineup.png` is the strict character reference.  
Primary request: Create one eye-catching vertical thumbnail for a comedic skit about vibe coding and a broken AI-generated SaaS app.  
Scene/backdrop: Use a bold, simplified office backdrop with a bright yellow-orange burst behind the characters and a dark laptop screen for contrast.  
Subject: On the left, show the navy-suit Investor with neat black hair, white shirt, and orange tie holding a smartphone toward the viewer. The phone clearly displays a large red `$4,000` charge alert. In the center, show the white-hoodie Vibe Coder with messy black hair presenting a laptop proudly. The laptop displays a broken dashboard with red warning marks, one oversized golden lock symbol, and one glowing green payment check mark. On the right, show the teal-sweater Senior Engineer with brown side-parted hair and rectangular black glasses doing a clear facepalm.  
Style/medium: Preserve the approved clean flat 2D stick-figure cartoon style: crisp black outlines, rounded white faces, dot eyes, simple expressive mouths, flat clothing blocks, and bright colors.  
Composition/framing: 9:16 portrait thumbnail. Use close, large character faces and upper bodies. Make the investor's shocked expression and phone readable at small size. Keep the text in the upper portion with generous padding.  
Text (verbatim): `VIBE CODING FAIL`  
Typography: Huge bold uppercase sans-serif lettering, white and red with a thick black outline and subtle red shadow. Keep every word fully visible and correctly spelled.  
Constraints: Use exactly the three recurring characters with their locked wardrobe designs. Keep the visual hierarchy simple and dramatic.  
Avoid: extra characters, tiny details, photorealism, 3D rendering, social-media UI chrome, watermarks, logos, long text, cropped headline, and misspelled text.
