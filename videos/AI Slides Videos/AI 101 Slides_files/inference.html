11 — 2 of 2 Phases: Inference
Inference is using what was learned.
This is what happens every time you press Send.
Training
Going to school.
·When: once, before the product ships
·Cost: millions of dollars in compute
·Time: weeks or months
·Output: a trained model (a big file of numbers)
·Who pays: the AI lab
Inference
Using the Knowledge.
·When: every time you send a message
·Cost: a fraction of a cent
·Time: seconds (or less)
·Output: a single answer
·Who pays: the app provider (or you)
A trained model can't "learn" mid‑conversation. It just runs.
