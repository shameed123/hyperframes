The ladder of intelligence
AI, AGI, and ASI
Think of them as levels: useful tools today, human-level general ability, then beyond-human intelligence.
AI
Current State of Intelligence.
Systems that perform useful intelligent work, usually within a bounded job or workflow.
Still need human intervention.
Singularity
AGI
Artificial General Intelligence.
Able to learn, reason, and adapt across many domains like a capable human.
ASI
Artificial Super Intelligence.
Better than humans at most or all cognitive tasks, including science and strategy.
AGI and ASI are similar because both are general intelligence. The difference is level: AGI matches humans; ASI surpasses us.
