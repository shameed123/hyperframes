10 — 1 of 2 Phases: Training
Training is practice with feedback.
Done once, in advance. Expensive. Months of compute. Billions of examples.
Machine Learning
1 Show example
"Here is a photo. The correct label is cat."
2 Model guesses
"I think it's a dog." (Wrong — for now.)
3 Measure error
"You were off by this much." (the "loss")
4 Nudge weights
Tiny adjustments so next time the guess leans toward "cat".
Repeat this loop billions of times. That's training.
