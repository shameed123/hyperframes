31 — What you can use it for today
Six honest, everyday wins.
Writing
Beat the blank page.
Draft emails, edit for tone, summarise long threads. Best as a first draft you then refine.
Research
A patient explainer.
"Explain the IRA rollover rules like I'm 12." Ask follow‑ups until it clicks.
Analysis
Talk to your data.
Paste a spreadsheet, ask for trends and outliers. Always sanity‑check the numbers.
Learning
A 24/7 tutor.
Practice a language, drill flashcards, walk through a math problem step by step.
Planning
Brainstorm partner.
Trip itineraries, gift ideas, weekly meal plans. It's tireless and won't judge a bad idea.
Quick tasks
The chore eraser.
Rewrite, translate, convert formats, format a CSV. Small stuff that used to take 20 minutes.
Rule of thumb: any task that starts with "draft," "summarise," "translate," or "explain."
