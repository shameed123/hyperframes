15 — Two knobs
Prompts & temperature
Prompt · "what you type"
The model's whole world is the prompt.
Better prompts give it more to work with. Three habits that pay off:
① Give context. "I'm a 7th‑grade teacher. Explain it to that age group."
② Specify format. "Reply as 3 bullet points, ≤15 words each."
③ Show an example. "Here's the style I want. Match it."
Temperature · "creativity dial"
Boring vs. brave.
When the model picks the next token, temperature controls how willing it is to pick a less obvious one.
0.0 · safe1.0 · default1.5+ · wild
Low — facts, code, structured output
Mid — chat, explanation, summaries
High — brainstorms, poems, names
