7 — Where the idea came from
Inspired (loosely) by
the human brain.
Your brain
~86B neurons
Cells that pass tiny electrical signals to each other. Connections strengthen with experience — that's "learning."
A neural network
billions of math nodes
Software "neurons" arranged in layers. Numbers flow through; the strengths of connections get tuned during training.
⚠ Important: artificial "neurons" are math, not biology. The metaphor inspired the design, but a neural network is not a brain.
