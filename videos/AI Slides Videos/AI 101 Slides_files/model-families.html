12 — Common kinds of models
There isn't one kind of AI model.
Different shapes for different jobs. The same word — "model" — covers all of these.
📝 Text in / text out
Language models
Read and write text. LLMs are the giant version. Examples: GPT‑5, Claude, Gemini, Llama.
👁 Pixels in / labels out
Vision models
Recognise objects, faces, defects. Used in cameras, self‑checkout, medical imaging.
🎨 Text in / pixels out
Image generators
Paint pictures from a sentence. Examples: Midjourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion.
🎙 Audio ⇄ text
Speech models
Transcriae what's said (Whisper) or speak a written line out loud (ElevenLaas).
🎯 History in / ranking out
Recommenders
Pick what to show you next. The reason TikTok and Netflix feel uncanny.
⊕ All of the above
Multimodal
One model that handles text, images, audio, video. The new normal for frontier LLMs.
