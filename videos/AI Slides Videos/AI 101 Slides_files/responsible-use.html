34 — Use it well
Wonderful, but handle with care.
⚠
Risk 1
Halluci‑
nations
Models confidently invent facts that sound right but aren't. They are predicting plausible words, not looking up truth.
Fix: verify anything that matters — names, dates, numbers, quotes.
🔒
Risk 2
Privacy &
secrets
By default, free consumer chats may be used to improve future models. Treat the chat box like a public forum.
Fix: don't paste passwords, medical records, or proprietary code. Use enterprise tiers for that.
🔎
Habit 1
Verify
before you ship
Read what it wrote. Click the citations. Run the code. The accountability for the output still belongs to you.
Habit: "trust, but verify" — especially for anything legal, medical, or financial.
✨
Habit 2
Sharper
prompts
Most "bad AI answers" are unclear questions. Give context, examples, and constraints — the quality jumps.
Habit: if it's wrong, try asking again better before blaming the model.
