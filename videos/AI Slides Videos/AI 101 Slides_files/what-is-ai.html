5 — What "AI" really means
AI = software that
learns patterns from data.
Traditional software
A human writes the rules.
if temperature > 30:
  turn_on_AC()
Predictable. Brittle. Only as smart as the rules.
AI software
The rules are learned.
Show 10,000 photos labeled "cat" / "not cat".
→ Model learns what "cat" looks like.
Flexible. Probabilistic. Can handle messy inputs no human rule‑set could.
