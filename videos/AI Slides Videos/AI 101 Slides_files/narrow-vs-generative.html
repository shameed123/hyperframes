6 — Two flavors of AI
Narrow AI vs. Generative AI
Both are real AI. They were built for different jobs.
🎯 Narrow AI · classifies, predicts
One job,
done very well.
Trained to answer one kind of question. Outputs a number, label, or decision.
Spam filter — "spam" or "not spam"
Face unlock — "match" or "no match"
Netflix picks — "rank these 10 shows"
✨ Generative AI · creates
Produces
brand‑new content.
Outputs text, images, audio or code that didn't exist before.
ChatGPT — writes a paragraph
Midjourney — paints an image
GitHub Copilot — drafts code
