14 — Tokens & context window
Models read in tokens, not letters.
The sentence:
"Artificial intelligence is wonderful."
Becomes 7 tokens:
Artificial  intelligence  is  wonder ful .
≈ A token is roughly ¾ of an English word. Spaces and punctuation count.
Context window
The model's short‑term memory.
It can only "see" so many tokens at once — typically 128k to 2M. Once you exceed it, the oldest tokens scroll out of view and are forgotten.
How much is that?
Roughly speaking…
1k tokens ≈ one page of a book
128k tokens ≈ a 300‑page novel
2M tokens ≈ a small library wing
