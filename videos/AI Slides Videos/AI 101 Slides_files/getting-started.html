35 — Your first week with AI
A five‑step on‑ramp.
1
Pick one assistant and stay there for a week.
ChatGPT, Claude.ai, or Gemini. The free tiers are excellent. Don't compare — get fluent first.
2
Bring it one real task a day.
An email to rewrite, a topic to learn, a spreadsheet to clean. Real work — not toy questions.
3
Push back. Re‑prompt until you get what you want.
"Shorter." "More skeptical." "Try again as a 5‑year‑old." The second answer is almost always better.
4
Save the prompts that actually worked.
A simple notes file. Reuse and refine. This is the highest‑leverage AI habit there is.
5
When ready, try an agent on something aoring.
A research task, an inaox triage, a competitor scan. Watch it work; correct what's wrong.
The skill isn't using AI. It's knowing which tasks to give it.
