# Thumbnail Prompt — Fable & Mythos Citizenship Restriction

**Use case:** YouTube Short / Reel / TikTok cover for an AI-news explainer.
**Asset type:** 9:16 vertical raster thumbnail (1080x1920).
**Primary request:** Generate a polished, high-contrast light-theme tech-news
thumbnail with the headline text baked into the image, spelled exactly, large
and phone-readable, never clipped.

**Scene / backdrop:** Bright, modern AI-editorial backdrop — pale blue-to-white
gradient with soft blue, pink, and orange glow blooms and faint circuit lines.
Clean, premium, "breaking news" energy.

**Subject:** A glowing AI model lock/shield icon split by a U.S. passport and a
government "RESTRICTED" stamp. To one side, two pill badges read "FABLE 5" and
"MYTHOS 5" with red "DISABLED" status marks. Suggest the idea of an identity /
citizenship checkpoint gating access to AI.

**Style / medium:** Glossy 3D glassmorphism, frosted translucent cards, subtle
depth shadows, crisp vector iconography.

**Composition / framing:** Headline occupies the top third in a heavy display
typeface. Central visual subject in the middle. Badges lower third. Generous
margins so nothing is clipped on a phone screen.

**Lighting / mood:** Bright, tense, authoritative. Cool blues with warning-red
accents.

**Color palette:** #1E8BFF blue, #FF4FA3 pink, #FF9D2E orange, #DC2626 warning
red, white/pale-blue base.

**Text (verbatim, baked in):** PROVE YOUR CITIZENSHIP TO USE AI?

**Secondary text (verbatim, baked in):** FABLE 5 & MYTHOS 5 DISABLED

**Constraints:** Headline must be one clean block, perfectly legible at phone
size, correctly spelled, not overlapping the subject. Keep it a single raster
image from the image model — do not composite text later.

**Avoid:** Tiny text, cluttered backgrounds, gibberish/garbled letters, real
politicians' faces, real government seals, dark-mode dashboards, watermarks,
more than two badges.
