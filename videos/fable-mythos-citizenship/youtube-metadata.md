## Description

A U.S. government order just pushed Anthropic to disable its two most powerful
AI models — Fable 5 and Mythos 5 — and the detail everyone is missing is *who*
the restriction targets.

According to Reuters, Anthropic said the government ordered it to suspend access
to these models for **foreign nationals**, citing national security concerns.
That is not a normal country ban. A normal ban blocks users *outside* America.
This targets nationality — meaning a non-citizen physically inside the U.S.
could still be restricted.

Anthropic has not announced a citizenship-verification system. But if the rule
is based on nationality instead of location, a simple location check may not be
enough — and some experts now expect users may eventually have to prove
citizenship before Fable 5 or Mythos 5 return.

This could affect foreign students, visa holders, international workers,
researchers, and even employees inside AI companies. Anthropic says other models
are unaffected and it wants access restored as soon as possible — but right now
there is no public timeline.

So is this a temporary national-security pause, or the start of an era where the
most powerful AI is treated like restricted technology?
