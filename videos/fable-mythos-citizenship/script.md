# Script: Fable & Mythos Citizenship Restriction

**Slug:** fable-mythos-citizenship
**Duration:** ~90 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 YouTube Short / Reel / TikTok
**Approved:** 2026-06-14

---

You may soon need to prove your citizenship just to use the most powerful AI models.

Anthropic just disabled Fable 5 and Mythos 5 after a U.S. government order.

And according to Reuters, Anthropic said the government ordered it to suspend access to these models for foreign nationals, citing national security concerns.

That detail is extremely important. Because this is not just a normal country ban. It is not simply saying, "block users outside America." It targets foreign nationals.

That means even someone physically inside the United States could still be restricted if they are not a U.S. citizen.

Now, to be clear, Anthropic has not officially announced a citizenship verification system yet. But if the rule is based on nationality instead of location, then location checks may not be enough.

That is why some experts now expect users may eventually have to prove citizenship before Fable 5 or Mythos 5 can come back.

Think about how big of a shift that would be. AI access could move from "log in and pay" to "prove who you are and what country you belong to."

This could affect foreign students, visa holders, international workers, researchers, and even employees inside AI companies.

Anthropic says other models are not affected, and it wants to restore access as soon as possible. But right now, there is no public timeline for when Fable 5 and Mythos 5 will return.

So the real question is: Is this a temporary national security pause, or are we entering a new era where the most powerful AI models are treated like restricted technology?
