# Motion Graphics Prompts

## Global Direction

Create a 9:16 YouTube Short in HyperFrames with a light, high-energy glossary style. Use a soft white-blue background, electric blue, hot pink, and orange accents. The animation should be kinetic and polished: 3D glossary cards fly in, icons pop forward, small artifacts orbit and burst, and every term has a visual metaphor. Keep the viewer anchored with large readable text and fast glossary popups.

Persistent scene system:

- Background: soft `#F7FBFF`, faint 3D grid floor, drifting token chips, low-opacity blue data lines, small pink/orange spark marks.
- Main recurring object: a floating 3D glossary card that changes shape, flips, rotates, or explodes into the next term.
- Captions: short kinetic subtitles at the bottom, never more than 3-5 words at once.
- Transitions: whip-pan cards, zoom-through icons, token bursts, glass-card flips, and camera push-ins.
- Energy: playful but premium, like a bright Apple keynote mixed with a punchy educational Short.

## Scene 1: Hook, 0-3s

Voiceover: "AI words sound complicated. They're mostly just normal ideas wearing a lab coat."

Motion prompt:
Start on a blank soft white-blue canvas. A swarm of tiny confusing AI acronyms, "LLM", "RAG", "TOKENS", "AGENTS", "MODEL", tumbles toward the viewer in 3D, then freezes midair. A glossy lab coat icon snaps onto a big central title card. The acronyms untangle into the headline "AI TERMS, PLAIN ENGLISH" with electric blue letters, hot pink accent underline, and orange sparkle hits. Use a fast camera push-in, slight 3D card tilt, and a final satisfying bounce.

Glossary popup:
"AI TERMS, PLAIN ENGLISH"

Icon/artifacts:
3D lab coat, floating acronym chips, magnifying glass, small sparkle strokes.

Transition out:
The title card splits into three stacked glossary cards and whips left, revealing the first term.

## Scene 2: AI, 3-7s

Voiceover: "AI means software that can do tasks we usually associate with people, like writing, seeing, sorting, or deciding."

Motion prompt:
A big blue "AI" cube drops into the center and unfolds into four mini action panels: pencil for writing, eye for seeing, stacked boxes for sorting, and checkmark path for deciding. Each icon flies out from the cube on orange motion trails, circles the frame, then locks into a clean glossary card. Make the card rotateY slightly as it lands, like a physical flashcard.

Glossary popup:
"AI = software that does smart tasks"

Icon/artifacts:
AI cube, pencil, eye, sorting stack, decision checkmark, mini orbit rings.

Transition out:
The AI cube transforms into a glowing engine core, spinning forward.

## Scene 3: Model, 7-12s

Voiceover: "A model is the engine. It has learned patterns from tons of examples."

Motion prompt:
The screen becomes a bright 3D engine diagram, not mechanical-heavy but playful and clean. Colored example cards fly in from all directions: text, image, chart, question, answer. They feed into the engine, which lights up in blue and orange. Pink pattern lines trace between the example cards, forming a web that compresses into the word "MODEL." The glossary popup appears as a stamped label on the engine.

Glossary popup:
"Model = pattern engine"

Icon/artifacts:
Engine core, example cards, pattern web, glowing bolts, data trails.

Transition out:
The engine spins faster, then emits chat bubbles that fill the next scene.

## Scene 4: LLM, 12-17s

Voiceover: "An LLM, or large language model, is a model that predicts words really well. That's why it can chat."

Motion prompt:
Create a cascade of floating word tiles. The tiles move like a prediction chain: "The", "AI", "can", then three possible next words appear as small 3D options. The correct next word pops forward in hot pink while the others shrink away. A large chat bubble forms from the tiles and flips into a glossy card reading "LLM." Make the motion feel clever and rhythmic.

Glossary popup:
"LLM = word prediction machine"

Icon/artifacts:
Word tiles, chat bubble, prediction arrows, small probability bars.

Transition out:
The chat bubble becomes a cursor prompt box and slides toward the viewer.

## Scene 5: Prompt, 17-22s

Voiceover: "A prompt is your instruction. Better prompt, better answer. Like giving directions to a very fast intern."

Motion prompt:
Show a floating keyboard and cursor field in 3D perspective. A messy instruction scribble enters from the left and produces a tiny confused answer card. Then the instruction gets rewritten as a crisp prompt, highlighted in blue and pink, and the answer card grows clean, organized, and confident. Add a tiny fast intern avatar icon zipping across the screen with orange speed lines, carrying the response card.

Glossary popup:
"Prompt = your instruction"

Icon/artifacts:
Keyboard, cursor, instruction field, answer card, fast helper icon, speed trails.

Transition out:
The typed letters break apart into colorful token chips.

## Scene 6: Tokens, 22-27s

Voiceover: "Tokens are tiny chunks of text. Not always full words. The AI reads and writes in tokens."

Motion prompt:
The sentence on screen fractures into rounded 3D chips. Some chips are whole words, some are partial word pieces, and some are punctuation. They bounce gently, then stream into a blue scanner beam labeled "AI reads this." Make the tokens feel tactile: little bevels, shadows, and sticker-like colors. Use a burst of chips that fly toward camera before settling into the glossary card.

Glossary popup:
"Tokens = text chunks"

Icon/artifacts:
Token chips, scanner beam, small text fragments, chip counter.

Transition out:
The token chips stack into a rectangular memory window.

## Scene 7: Context Window, 27-32s

Voiceover: "The context window is the AI's short-term memory. If it gets full, older stuff falls out."

Motion prompt:
Build a floating browser-like window labeled "Context." Token chips fill the window from left to right. As it reaches capacity, older chips gently slide out the back edge and fall away with a soft blur. Use a progress meter at the top that fills in orange. The glossary popup should snap onto the window like a bright sticky note.

Glossary popup:
"Context window = short-term memory"

Icon/artifacts:
Memory window, token chips, capacity meter, falling old notes, sticky note label.

Transition out:
The memory window flips like a page into an open-book training scene.

## Scene 8: Training And Inference, 32-37s

Voiceover: "Training data is what helped teach the model. Inference is when it uses what it learned to answer you."

Motion prompt:
Split the frame into two dynamic halves. Left side: an open 3D book with example cards flying into it, labeled "Training = learning." Right side: the model engine outputs one clean answer card, labeled "Inference = answering." Animate the split with a diagonal glass divider that slides into place. Keep the labels big and simple, with blue for learning and orange for answering.

Glossary popup:
"Training = learning / Inference = answering"

Icon/artifacts:
Open book, example cards, engine output, answer card, diagonal divider.

Transition out:
The answer card glitches slightly and turns into a warning sign for hallucination.

## Scene 9: Hallucination, 37-43s

Voiceover: "A hallucination is when AI sounds confident but gets the facts wrong. Basically: fluent nonsense."

Motion prompt:
A polished answer card enters confidently with a shiny blue checkmark, then the checkmark wobbles and flips into a hot pink warning triangle. The answer text looks smooth at first, then several words detach, float upward, and reveal question marks underneath. Use a playful "too confident" stamp that slams onto the card, followed by the glossary popup. Keep it fun, not scary.

Glossary popup:
"Hallucination = confident mistake"

Icon/artifacts:
Answer card, warning triangle, question marks, wobbling checkmark, "confident" stamp.

Transition out:
Question marks become dot clusters and connect into a vector map.

## Scene 10: Embeddings, 43-49s

Voiceover: "Embeddings turn meaning into numbers, so AI can find ideas that are similar, not just exact words."

Motion prompt:
Show a 3D field of floating dots, each dot representing an idea. Words like "car", "vehicle", "road", "banana", and "recipe" appear as tiny tags. Similar ideas pull near each other with elastic blue lines; unrelated ones drift away. Then the tags collapse into clean number vectors, like `[0.42, 0.18, 0.91]`, while the dot cluster forms a glowing map. Make this feel beautiful and airy.

Glossary popup:
"Embeddings = meaning as numbers"

Icon/artifacts:
Vector dots, similarity lines, word tags, number arrays, soft depth field.

Transition out:
The vector map folds into a stack of documents.

## Scene 11: RAG, 49-54s

Voiceover: "RAG means the AI checks outside sources before answering, like open-book test mode."

Motion prompt:
Create an "open-book test" moment. A question card lands in the center. The AI card reaches out with animated search beams to a stack of documents, webpages, and notes. Relevant source cards fly back and snap behind the answer card like citations. Use blue beams for search, pink highlights for relevant facts, and orange tabs for sources. The final answer card should look more trustworthy and grounded.

Glossary popup:
"RAG = AI + reference material"

Icon/artifacts:
Question card, book, document stack, web page cards, search beams, citation tabs.

Transition out:
The source cards fold into a tool belt around a cursor icon.

## Scene 12: Agent, 54-59s

Voiceover: "An agent is AI that can take steps: search, click, write, check, and keep going toward a goal."

Motion prompt:
Animate a bright cursor-arrow character or 3D pointer moving along a goal path. It triggers five mini action icons in sequence: search magnifier, click cursor, write pencil, checkmark, loop arrow. Each action icon pops out as a tiny 3D artifact, then snaps into a timeline path. The path ends at a glowing goal flag. Make this scene feel like momentum and autonomy without feeling chaotic.

Glossary popup:
"Agent = AI that takes actions"

Icon/artifacts:
Cursor character, magnifier, click ripple, pencil, checkmark, loop arrow, goal flag.

Transition out:
The action path curls into a bright circular frame for the end card.

## Scene 13: End Card, 59-60s

Voiceover: "That's the cheat sheet. AI is less magic when the words stop being weird."

Motion prompt:
All glossary cards from the video fly back into frame in a quick circular orbit, then stack into one clean deck labeled "AI GLOSSARY." The deck lands with a soft bounce. A large CTA appears: "SAVE THIS GLOSSARY." Add a final burst of blue, pink, and orange confetti chips, then freeze on a clean readable frame.

Glossary popup:
"Save this glossary"

Icon/artifacts:
Glossary card deck, orbiting term cards, confetti chips, bookmark icon.

Final frame:
Large centered "SAVE THIS GLOSSARY" with small term chips around it: AI, Model, LLM, Prompt, Tokens, Context, RAG, Agent.
