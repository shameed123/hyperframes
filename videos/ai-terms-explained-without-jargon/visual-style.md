# Visual Style: Pop Glossary Lab

## Style Prompt

A bright, premium explainer style for AI glossary terms: light canvas, crisp editorial typography, floating 3D glossary cards, playful icons, and fast kinetic transitions. The video should feel like a clean tech classroom crossed with a colorful product launch. Every term gets a satisfying visual metaphor: cards flip, tokens burst, windows expand, particles connect, and action icons fly across the frame.

## Colors

- Background: `#F7FBFF` soft cool white
- Ink: `#162033` deep blue-black for primary text
- Electric blue: `#1D6CFF` for AI/data motion, outlines, and key highlights
- Hot pink: `#FF3EA5` for popups, emphasis bursts, and contrast accents
- Solar orange: `#FF8A1D` for icons, arrows, energy trails, and CTA moments
- Mist blue: `#DDEBFF` for glass panels, shadows, and soft depth layers

## Typography

- Headline: `Inter Tight` or `Inter`, 800-900 weight, oversized and compact
- Body / glossary labels: `Inter`, 500-700 weight, clear and highly readable
- Term badges: uppercase, 700 weight, short labels only

## Motion Rules

- Use fast, polished entrances: 0.35-0.6s with `back.out`, `expo.out`, or `power4.out`.
- Use overlapping motion so a new icon starts entering before the previous popup fully exits.
- Make glossary cards feel physical: 3D tilt, flip, rotateY, small z-depth shadows, and perspective.
- Use ambient background motion: slow orbit rings, drifting mini tokens, soft grid shimmer, and low-opacity data trails.
- Keep captions legible: no more than 3-5 words per caption beat.

## 3D Language

- Glossary cards are chunky glass-like panels with soft bevel illusion, layered shadows, and subtle rotateX/rotateY.
- Icons appear as colorful 3D stickers: brain, engine, chat bubble, keyboard, token chips, memory window, book, warning sign, vector dots, document stack, cursor/agent.
- Use flying artifacts as transitions: cards sweep past camera, icons whoosh offscreen, text chunks spiral into the next term.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use a dark neon cyberpunk look.
- Do not make flat static slides.
- Do not use generic blue-purple gradients as the main visual system.
- Do not overcrowd the frame so definitions become hard to read.
- Do not let decorative motion compete with the glossary text.
