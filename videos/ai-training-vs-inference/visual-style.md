# Visual Style: Slide-Native AI Classroom

## Style Prompt

Keep the original AI 101 slides as the visual authority. The vertical video should feel like a polished instructor walking the viewer through a large classroom slide: warm off-white canvas, precise blue and pink teaching accents, smooth editorial camera moves, a restrained cursor, and translucent highlighter boxes. Avoid separate infographic scenes unless needed for the course CTA. The avatar remains a compact bottom-right guide so the slide content stays legible.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#FBF8F3`
- Primary ink: `#2F3744`
- Secondary ink: `#3A4A63`
- Training accent: `#EC4899`
- Inference accent: `#2563EB`
- Teaching highlight: `#F97316`

## Typography

- Slide content: preserve the source slide typography.
- Video overlay labels and captions: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace, for a precise classroom register.
- CTA headline: `Instrument Serif`, serif, matching the slides' expressive teaching voice.

## Motion

- Use smooth camera pushes and pans across the horizontal slides inside the vertical crop.
- Move the cursor only when a spoken phrase points to a specific slide region.
- Use translucent rounded highlighter boxes and short underline sweeps.
- Use a gentle push transition when moving from training to inference.
- Keep the avatar picture-in-picture steady with only a subtle float.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not cover the source slide with unrelated motion-graphics cards.
- Do not use dark tech backgrounds, neon grids, or glitch effects.
- Do not move the slide camera continuously; allow readable holds.
- Do not place captions over the avatar or the highlighted slide region.
