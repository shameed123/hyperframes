Google just gave Project Genie a real-world anchor: Street View.

Until now, Genie was already wild: type a prompt, and it could generate an interactive world you can move through.

But at Google I/O 2026, Google said Genie can now start from Street View imagery. In plain English: pick a real place, and the model can build a playable, AI-generated version with a creative twist.

That matters because this is not just video generation. It is closer to an AI simulator. You are not only watching pixels; you are exploring a space, changing perspective, and testing what might happen next.

Google says the feature is rolling out gradually to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers, and it points to bigger use cases: games, virtual production, education, robotics, and self-driving car training.

The catch is important: these worlds are generated, not perfect copies of reality. So think less "teleport anywhere exactly," and more "turn real places into explorable simulations."

Still, the direction is huge. AI is moving from making images, to making videos, to making worlds you can step inside.
