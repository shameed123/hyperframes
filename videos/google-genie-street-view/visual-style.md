# Visual Style

Bright AI field-report style with mostly blue and white, crisp editorial type, hot pink and orange accents, Street View/map motifs, and vivid simulation overlays.

Use shallow 3D movement, oversized mobile-phone source scrolls, map pins, generated-world rings, fast diagonal beams, and high-energy UI labels. Keep the avatar in cropped picture-in-picture for most sections, with one larger feature moment near the intro.
