## Research Brief

**Top Story**
Google connected Project Genie with Street View, letting the AI world model create explorable environments grounded in real-world imagery.

**Why It Matters**
This is a clean short-video story because viewers instantly understand the leap: AI is moving from images and video toward interactive spaces. The strongest angle is: "Google is turning real places into AI-generated worlds you can explore."

**Verified Facts**

- Google announced the Street View capability for Project Genie on May 19, 2026.
- Google says Genie connects generative world-building with real-world Street View imagery.
- Google says Project Genie, including the Street View capability, is gradually rolling out to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers globally, age 18+.
- Google DeepMind describes Genie 3 as a real-time, interactive world model that can generate photorealistic worlds from a text description.
- Reputable tech coverage reported the announcement as a Street View-grounded simulation capability.
