# Thumbnail Prompt: Fable 5 Release

## Use Case
YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Instagram Reels thumbnail for AI news video about Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 release.

## Asset Type
9:16 vertical thumbnail image (1080x1920)

## Primary Request
A dramatic, high-tech thumbnail featuring a glowing AI core or brain-like structure in deep red and orange tones, with glass morphic card elements floating around it. The design should convey breakthrough power and exclusivity.

## Scene/Backdrop
Dark gradient background transitioning from deep red (#b43232) at the top to dark blue (#1a1a2e) at the bottom. Subtle circuit-like patterns and glowing particles in the background.

## Subject
A central glowing AI core/processor emitting red and orange light, with translucent glass cards floating around it showing model names and benchmark percentages. The Fable 5 name should be prominent.

## Style/Medium
Glass morphic / frosted glass aesthetic, 3D digital art, tech editorial style, high contrast for phone screens.

## Composition/Framing
Center-weighted composition with the main headline at the top and supporting elements below. The AI core should be the focal point with light rays emanating outward.

## Lighting/Mood
Dramatic, high-energy, urgent lighting with red/orange glow from the center. High-tech, announcement-style mood.

## Color Palette
- Primary: Deep red (#b43232), bright red (#ff4444)
- Secondary: Electric blue (#2458ff), orange (#ff8a21)
- Background: Dark blue-black gradient
- Text: White with red accents

## Text (verbatim)
**Headline:** "FABLE 5"
**Secondary:** "MYTHOS-CLASS AI"
**Tagline:** "80.3% SWE-Bench"

## Constraints
- Headline text must be baked into the image
- All text must be phone-readable (large, high contrast)
- No clipping of headline text
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (1080x1920)

## Avoid
- Small or thin fonts
- Low contrast text
- Cluttered design
- Dark text on dark backgrounds
- Generic AI imagery (robots, stock photos)
