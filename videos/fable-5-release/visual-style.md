# Visual Style: Fable 5 Release

## Theme
High-tech AI announcement with dramatic reveals and 3D flying elements. Glass morphic / frosted glass aesthetic with liquid glass cards.

## Color Palette
- Primary: Deep red (#b04d00, #ff4444) for Fable 5 branding - "red alert" theme
- Accent Blue: #2458ff for Anthropic/Claude branding
- Accent Pink: #ff3aa8 for highlights and energy
- Accent Orange: #ff8a21 for warm accents
- Background: Light pastel tech canvas with white/soft-silver base
- Glass cards: Translucent layered fill with white rim highlights, inner shine

## Visual Elements
- 3D flying glass cards with depth and perspective
- Model tier ladder (Haiku → Sonnet → Opus → Fable) with ascending visual
- Benchmark scoreboard with animated bars
- Calendar/timeline visualization for free window
- Usage meter animations
- Price comparison cards
- Dramatic entrance/exit animations with 3D rotations

## Typography
- Headlines: Archivo Black or similar heavy display face
- Technical labels: IBM Plex Mono or Consolas
- Large, high-contrast text for phone readability

## Motion Style
- 3D card entrances: fly in from depth with rotateX/rotateY
- Floating animations: subtle y-axis drift with rotation
- Scene transitions: wipe bands with blue/pink/orange gradients
- Glass card effects: backdrop blur, inner shadows, rim highlights
