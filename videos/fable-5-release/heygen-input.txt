Anthropic just released Claude Fable 5… and this is not a normal AI model update.

This is their first public Mythos-class model — a new tier above Opus.

Think of it like this: Sonnet is fast, Opus is powerful… but Fable is the "bring me the impossible project" model.

And the numbers are spicy.
On SWE-Bench Pro, Fable hits 80.3%.
Opus 4.8? 69.2%.
GPT-5.5? 58.6%.

That is not a small bump. That is Fable walking into the coding arena and flipping the table.

Anthropic says Stripe tested it on a 50-million-line Ruby codebase, and Fable finished a migration in one day that would have taken a full engineering team over two months by hand.

Vision also got a serious upgrade. On GDP.pdf, a document-vision benchmark, Fable scored 29.8%, beating GPT-5.5 at 24.9% and Opus 4.8 at 22.5%.

And here's the part most people will miss: Fable 5 is only temporarily included for paid Claude subscribers.

From June 9 to June 22, Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise users can try Fable 5 through Claude.ai.

But it still counts as 2x usage, because this model is compute-hungry.

After June 23, Fable 5 is expected to require usage credits unless Anthropic has enough capacity to extend access.

And on API, the price is serious: $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens — double Opus 4.8.

So Fable 5 is stronger than Opus, more expensive than GPT-5.5, temporarily available to paid users… and then it moves closer to pay-as-you-go pricing.

The real question is: did Anthropic just release the most powerful public AI model — or give us a limited-time demo of something most people won't be able to afford?
