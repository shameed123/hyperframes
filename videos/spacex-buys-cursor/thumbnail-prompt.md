# Thumbnail Prompt — SpaceX Buys Cursor

## Image generation prompt

Hyper-realistic digital illustration, 1280×720 (16:9), YouTube thumbnail style.

**Composition:**  
Split background — left half deep space navy (#0a0e1a) with subtle star field, right half electric blue tech grid (#0f1f3d). A glowing crack/fracture runs diagonally down the center where the two halves meet, radiating cyan and orange light.

**Center focal element:**  
A giant cursor/caret symbol (the Cursor AI logo style — simple right-pointing triangle) rendered in chrome-metallic steel, slightly cracked, with SpaceX rocket exhaust flame blasting out the bottom of it. The rocket exhaust is bright orange-red.

**Left side:**  
Elon Musk's silhouette (dark, backlit, in dramatic billionaire pose — looking at his phone) against the space background. Subtle SpaceX/xAI logo watermark near him.

**Right side:**  
Glowing laptop screen showing VSCode-style code editor with Cursor branding. Multiple dev avatars (small icons of shocked/angry developer faces) float around it.

**Text overlays (baked into the image):**  
- Top-left: "$60 BILLION" in massive bold white Impact font with red drop shadow  
- Bottom-center: "ELON BOUGHT YOUR CODE EDITOR" in chunky white text with electric blue outline  
- Small badge top-right: red circle with "🚀 CURSOR" in white

**Mood:** Shocking, slightly dystopian, high-energy tech drama. Cinematic lighting.

- Cursor caret logo should be distressed/cracked to imply disruption
- Color palette: navy, electric blue, white, orange-red accent
