# Visual Style — spacex-buys-cursor

## Theme
Frosted glass, light high-tech AI editorial.

## Palette
- Base: pale blue/white (#f8fcff → #eaf5ff)
- Accent 1: electric blue (#1E8BFF) — SpaceX/tech
- Accent 2: hot pink/magenta (#FF4FA3) — danger/controversy
- Accent 3: amber/orange (#FF9D2E) — money/warning

## Typography
- Headlines: Archivo Black (bold, large)
- Data labels: IBM Plex Mono
- Body: Segoe UI / Aptos

## Card Style
Frosted glass with white rim, inner shine, radial color blooms, `backdrop-filter: blur(34px)`.
Cards fly in with 3D GSAP: `rotateY: -28`, `z: -220`, `back.out(1.65)` ease.
Cards fly out with `rotateX: -14`, `y: -72`, `power3.in`.

## B-Roll
- CNBC article page (mobile scroll)
- TechCrunch article page (mobile scroll)
- GeekWire article (desktop hero crop)

## Avatar Layout
- Scenes 1, 7 (hook + CTA): feature-top — large centered avatar window
- Scenes 2–6: PIP bottom-right over B-roll
