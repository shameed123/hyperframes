AI doesn't read text the way we do.

It reads your prompt as tokens: tiny chunks of words, spaces, and punctuation.

That's why this sentence doesn't become one thing. It gets broken into pieces the model can process.

Artificial. Intelligence. Is. Wonder. Ful. Period. Even the space before a word can become part of the token.

A good mental shortcut: one token is roughly three quarters of an English word. Not exact, but close enough for planning.

Now the important part: tokens are also how we measure the context window.

The context window is the model's short-term memory. It can only see so many tokens at once.

When the window gets too full, older tokens slide out of view. That's why very long chats can lose earlier details.

So when you prompt, think in tokens: what does the model need to see right now?
