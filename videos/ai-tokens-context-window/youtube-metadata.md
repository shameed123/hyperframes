## Title

AI Tokens Explained: Why ChatGPT Reads Tokens, Not Words

## Description

AI tokens explained in under a minute: models do not read text exactly like people do. They break prompts into tokens, which are small chunks of words, spaces, and punctuation. This short AI for beginners lesson shows how a sentence becomes token pieces, why a token is roughly three quarters of an English word, and how the context window works as the model's short-term memory.

When a ChatGPT conversation gets long, ChatGPT tokens are what fill the window. The model can only keep so many tokens in view at once. New tokens enter the context window, and older tokens can eventually slide out of view. That is why prompt engineering, conversation history, and context length matter when working with AI tools and large language models.
