# Thumbnail Brand

Series promise: make AI concepts feel instantly clear, visual, and worth tapping.

Click trigger: reveal the surprising mental model behind familiar AI terms. For this video, the hook is that AI reads tokens, not words.

Recurring motifs:

- oversized glossy 3D headline text;
- frosted-glass AI containers with physical token tiles;
- a visible transformation path from normal language into model-readable chunks.

Color and lighting:

- warm off-white base with pale blue haze;
- high-energy pink and orange accent glow;
- polished rim lights, bright glass reflections, and strong depth;
- no dark neon grid or muddy low-contrast background.

Text hierarchy:

- one huge phone-readable headline in the top half;
- one short badge or contradiction such as `NOT WORDS`;
- tiny labels only when they support the visual idea.

Banned elements:

- no avatar, logos, watermark, tiny UI, unrelated sample text, misspelled labels, or clutter.
